# Descriptive alignment diagnostic

Recorded 2026-09-13 Asia/Shanghai during revision, after the original Cluster and calibration results were known. This diagnostic responds to the request to separate spectral alignment from classification; it is exploratory, not a new test of efficacy and never selects a model.

Use the already fixed temporal, spatial, and corrected spatial feature definitions for Cluster. Estimate correction from the declared repeat-0 source mask only. For analysis, average the normalized bin probabilities within each specimen and each of the five nominal speeds, using cardinal directions, 500 mN, and both acquisition repeats. Report the mean generalized Jensen–Shannon divergence among the five speed distributions within a specimen, and the generalized Jensen–Shannon divergence among the 118 specimen centroids (each averaged across five speeds). Use natural logarithms; the respective bounds are log(5) and log(118). Report both components, all 118 specimen values, and their ratio where the denominator is nonzero. These are information-space descriptors, not accuracy estimates or causal tests. The coordinates/bands differ by representation, so absolute divergence differences alone do not prove better physical alignment or separability.

The same definitions may be applied to the external dataset only after its global model freeze and completed target evaluation, with all three speeds and the dataset's twelve class-balanced episode means. No target spectra or labels are used to fit a response. No threshold, interval or hypothesis claim will be selected based on these descriptors. Preserve all three methods regardless of direction.
