# Prospective operational clarifications, before classifier fitting

Responds to the separate internal protocol audit. Original v1 remains unchanged.

1. Motion flags are audit-only, not exclusions: derive central-window displacement speed as total endpoint displacement / elapsed time; report deviation from commanded speed and central stops of at least100ms with displacement speed below10% of commanded over100ms. Do not drop flagged records. Missing position data blocks a clean acquisition-quality claim but does not alter accelerometer classification labels. Accelerometer validity rules in v1 still govern exclusions. Require all118 specimen×held-speed cells and use identical valid acquisition sets for every method; report per-cell denominators. More than1% invalid scans, missing specimen/domain or missing entire held-speed cell stops formal inference.
2. Integrate a piecewise-linear PSD analytically at exact bin edges; do not sum isolated FFT bins. Fine output bins are correlated interpolations, not independent frequency observations. No extra Jacobian multiplies integrated spatial-bin masses.
3. H1 rule means a positive effect (CI lower bound >0) whose point estimate meets3pp. Claiming the true effect exceeds3pp would additionally require its lower bound >3pp; report both facts separately.
4. Ties: maximize mean source-fold macro accuracy, then smaller C, smaller absolute log(gamma_multiplier), smaller gamma_multiplier, smaller beta. For endpoint-union ties choose temporal(beta0). Retain all fold scores and candidate definitions.
5. Dual representation concatenates standardized blocks without pretending that pre-standardization division by sqrt2 changes weights; gamma scaling by total feature dimension is used. The division was redundant and is removed. A dimension/search-count difference remains a disclosed limitation.
6. Governing budget: up to6GB unique downloaded archive ranges, including position and metadata; repeated transfer failures are logged separately and total network bytes on broken TLS connections may not be measurable. Budget is a local engineering limit, not a purchased quota. Up to90minutes wall time for source model selection plus formal evaluation, measured separately from download/extraction. Priority: core repeat0, core repeat1, gain controls, distance controls, metadata perturbations. Planned controls left incomplete by timeout remain explicitly incomplete, never dropped from reporting. At most2 compute threads; 4 I/O download workers. Disk/unique ranges must stay within6GB; retries cannot lead to new unplanned data.
7. Constant-distance control is a sensitivity analysis, not full isolation: fixed time-domain Welch length changes spatial resolution with speed, and variable duration changes averaging variance. No strong causal explanation can be inferred from its result.
8. Protocol addendum and code/selected-model/feature/split hashes are sealed before target inference. Operational download resumption repairs do not change data selection, labels, hypotheses or models.
